How to report a problem

  1. Press the + button above.
  2. Take or upload a clear photo of the problem (required).
  3. Describe what is wrong in a sentence or two.
  4. Pick the category that fits best.
  5. Choose who to share it with (Dean, Registrar, Professor, Hostel warden or Other).
  6. To send it to one particular admin only, type that admin's email. Leave it empty to send it to the main admin.
  7. Choose the location, or tap Use my exact location.
  8. Press Submit report, then follow it here: Reported, Under process, Solved.
